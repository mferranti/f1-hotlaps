# Custom-content picture-in-picture to keep receiving in the background

Neither iOS nor Android lets an app keep a UDP socket open once it is in the background, so Packets stop arriving as soon as the player switches to another app between Races. We keep the app alive with picture-in-picture showing our own content (the status dot, the Packet rate graph, and the current rate): on Android the activity enters picture-in-picture, and on iOS a view is rendered into an `AVSampleBufferDisplayLayer` with a mix-with-others audio session so music keeps playing. A 30-minute Waiting limit closes it so the phone can suspend the app. Apple may reject custom-content picture-in-picture in App Review; we accept that risk because the app is installed only on personal devices through development builds or TestFlight, and it is the only way to receive in the background without a server.
